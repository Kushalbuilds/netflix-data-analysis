# Netflix Movies and TV Shows Data Analysis

## Project Overview
Analyzed a Netflix titles dataset using Python to understand content distribution, country representation, movie durations, and yearly content additions.

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas and NumPy
- Matplotlib and Seaborn
- Google Colab
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Key Findings

1. **Content distribution:** The dataset contains 6,131 movies (69.62%) and 2,676 TV Shows (30.38%), for a total of 8,807 titles.
2. **Country analysis:** The United States (listed as USA in the dataset) has the highest country count at 3,690, followed by India at 1,046 and the United Kingdom (listed as UK) at 806.
3. **Movie duration:** The average movie duration is 98.58 minutes, and the median is 98 minutes.
4. **Duration range:** The shortest recorded movie is *Silent* (3 minutes), while the longest recorded duration is *Black Mirror: Bandersnatch* (312 minutes).
5. **Yearly additions:** The highest overall number of additions occurred in 2019, with 2,016 titles.
6. **Movies vs TV Shows:** Movie additions peaked in 2019 with 1,424 titles. TV Show additions peaked in 2020 with 595 titles.

## Conclusion
The analysis shows that movies represent the majority of titles in this dataset. Movie runtimes are centered around 98–99 minutes, and yearly additions reached their highest overall level in 2019. Visualizations and summary statistics helped identify patterns and communicate findings.

## Limitations
- The dataset reflects a particular snapshot of Netflix titles, not necessarily the current catalog.
- A title can be associated with multiple countries.
- Missing values and unusual duration records may affect some analyses.
- The findings describe the dataset and do not establish causal relationships.
